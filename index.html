Privacy Policy for AIO Player

Developer: Abedelmassih
App name: AIO Player
Package name: com.abedelmassih.aioplayer
Effective date: November 17, 2026

In short: AIO Player keeps everything it saves only on your phone. It never sends, uploads or shares any data, it has no internet access, and uninstalling the app deletes everything it saved.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

AIO Player is an offline music and video player developed by Abedelmassih. If you have any question about this policy, contact us at: adhvictra@gmail.com.

2. Information the app saves

AIO Player saves the following information inside its own private storage on your phone:

  • your favorites and playlists,
  • your play history: how often and when you played each track,
  • your settings, such as dark mode, equalizer preset, music bitrate, screen refresh rate, and the order of your library tabs,
  • the equalizer band settings,
  • the lock screen control setting,
  • the library tab and album or artist you were viewing,
  • the queue of songs you were playing, and the position in the current song so playback can resume where you stopped,
  • a record that you agreed to this privacy policy.

This information is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not used to identify you. The app does not create accounts and does not ask you to sign in.

3. Media files on your device

The app reads the music and video files that are already on your phone so it can list and play them. It does not upload or change your files. Renaming and deleting a file happen only after you confirm in the system dialog. Sharing a file opens Android’s share menu, and the file is given to the app you choose.

4. Permissions and why we ask for them

  • Audio and video access (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, and on older Android versions READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): used only to find, list, and play the music and video files on your device.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): used to show playback controls while music plays and on the lock screen.
  • Background playback (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK, WAKE_LOCK): keeps music playing when the screen is off or the app is in the background.

You can turn off notification access at any time in your phone settings. The app keeps working for local playback without it.

5. No internet access and no data sharing

AIO Player does not request internet access. It cannot connect to the internet, and it cannot send any of the information described in this policy to us or to any third party. We do not sell, rent, or share information, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking services.

6. Removing your data

Everything AIO Player saves, including your favorites, playlists, play history, settings and resume position, is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can also delete it without uninstalling by opening your phone settings, choosing Apps, selecting AIO Player, and clearing its data. Your music and video files are not part of the app and are never removed by uninstalling AIO Player.

7. Backups

AIO Player is excluded from automatic backup. Its saved information is therefore not copied to your Google, Huawei or other cloud account. If you change phones, your saved information may not carry over, and you may need to start again with the default settings.

8. Links to other websites

The "Support developer" button in Settings is optional. Tapping it opens PayPal in your web browser. PayPal's own privacy policy governs anything you do on that website, and we do not receive your payment details.

9. Children's privacy

AIO Player does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it sends nothing to us.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at the same web address.
