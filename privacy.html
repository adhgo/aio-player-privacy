Privacy Policy for AIO Player

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

AIO Player ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

AIO Player is an offline media player designed to play audio and video files stored locally on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage analytics, or media files on external servers.

2. Device Permissions

Storage / Media Access: The app requests permission to read audio and video files on your device solely so it can scan, list, and play your local media inside the app. All file scanning happens locally on your phone.

3. Third-Party Links

The app includes an optional external support link ("Buy Me a Coffee"). Clicking this link opens your device's web browser to an external website governed by its own privacy policy.

4. Children's Privacy

Because AIO Player does not collect any personal data from users of any age, it complies with applicable children's privacy regulations.
